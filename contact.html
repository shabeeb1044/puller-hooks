FAQ

Common questions

Do you sell direct to anglers?

PULLER works primarily through dealers and distributors. Write to us and we will point you to a partner in your country, or talk about opening a new territory.

Can I request colour charts or samples?

Yes. Mention the line — for example FLASH STRIKER — and your market. We share catalogues and sample options with qualified buyers.

How do wholesale and MOQ work?

Minimums depend on the product and destination. Include estimated volume and destination port in your message for a clear quote.

How do I become an authorized dealer?

Use the dealer application. We review store details and follow up within one business day.
