PULLER Co., Ltd.

A leading fishing products manufacturer

PULLER Co., Ltd. is a leading manufacturer and exporter of high-quality fishing products, including high-carbon steel fishing hooks, stainless steel fishing hooks, hard-body lures (FLASH STRIKER), metal lures, and jigs.

With a strong commitment to quality, innovation, and performance, PULLER specializes in fishing products for both commercial and sport fishing. Our hooks and lures are designed to deliver high strength, exceptional durability, and reliable performance across a wide range of fishing environments and conditions.
