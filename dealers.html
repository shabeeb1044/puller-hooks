Dealer FAQ

Common questions

Who can become a PULLER dealer?

We work with established tackle shops, marine/outdoor retailers, online stores, and regional distributors. Tell us about your channel and market in the application — we prioritize partners who can represent the brand well.

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ depends on product line and destination. After we review your application, we share dealer pricing and minimums for the lines you want to stock.

Do you offer exclusive territories?

Coverage is discussed case by case. We aim for healthy distribution — strong partners in open markets, and clear guidance where we already have authorized dealers.

Can I get samples or a colour chart?

Yes. Qualified applicants can request catalogues, FLASH STRIKER hard-body lures, metal lures and jigs colour references, and sample options. Mention what you need in the form or email sales.
